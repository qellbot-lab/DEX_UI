# V2 Competitor and Product Framework Baseline

This file establishes the evidence set for the three later audit rounds. Competitors inform product clarity and trust patterns, not visual cloning or content import.

## Market References

| Product | Observed framework | V2 learning |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | select/build an Agent, fund a vault, activate | Keep the primary path obvious, but preserve QELL's simulation-first boundary and avoid money-deployment cues |
| Doko | build, simulate, compete; agent history and versioning | Strong comparator for QELL's simulation and leaderboard loop; show progress without game-like noise |
| MoTA | institutional multi-Agent team with defined roles and human approval | Make team composition and role accountability legible; keep human control visible |
| Portfolio Lab | strategy construction, optimization, paper validation, agent trading | Separate strategy quality from outcome and make validation a first-class step |
| NOFA | create, backtest, then live autonomous operation | Use progressive disclosure; do not expose execution complexity before the user chooses a scenario |
| APAIIR | research, portfolio, market; transparent measurable decisions | Result and runtime need traceable rationale rather than theatrical “thinking” copy |
| AgentAlpha | workspace, simulate first, operator oversight | Reinforce demo/paper boundaries and provide pause/review controls |
| Sylor | multi-Agent scenario variants and probabilistic outputs | Historical scenarios should communicate uncertainty, difficulty, and variants clearly |

## QELL Product Differentiation

QELL's strongest coherent loop is:

1. discover strategy personalities;
2. build a complementary Alpha Team;
3. stress-test the team against a recognizable historical event;
4. observe factor and execution choices;
5. review outcome and decision attribution;
6. compare progress against other teams.

The prototype should make this loop clearer than any individual dashboard.

## Audit Criteria

### Round 1 — Visual hierarchy and reference fidelity

- Does each route have one unmistakable focal judgment?
- Is hierarchy created by scale, spacing, material, and density?
- Does the prototype preserve reference-site restraint without copying its content?
- Are purple and generic AI motifs absent?

### Round 2 — Product-market and flow clarity

- Can a new user explain the journey after the home page?
- Can the user reach a meaningful simulation in a small number of decisions?
- Are team roles, scenario difficulty, and simulation-only boundaries explicit?
- Are rationale and attribution visible without exposing unverifiable chain-of-thought?

### Round 3 — Interaction, polish, and production readiness

- Do primary routes and controls work with realistic demo state?
- Are keyboard focus, contrast, overflow, reduced motion, and responsive behavior acceptable?
- Are loading, empty, maximum-team, and paused states covered?
- Are there console errors, dead controls, or visual regressions?
